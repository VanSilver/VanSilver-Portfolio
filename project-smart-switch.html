Project Details

Smart Switch
Smart Switch Touch Sqr V2

The Smart Switch project utilizes capacitive touch technology to replace traditional physical buttons, offering a smooth touch experience and an elegant design for smart homes.

Powered by the ESP32 microcontroller and MQTT protocol, the system provides a robust wireless connection, allowing users to easily control and monitor their devices remotely via a mobile application.
